# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Pizza Logs a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, or attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- malicious code, spam, intentional service disruption, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces. Project spaces include the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and official community channels.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a [GitHub private security advisory](https://github.com/CRSD-Lau/Pizza-Logs/security/advisories/new). Include relevant links, dates, context, and any immediate safety concern. Reports will be reviewed by the project maintainer and handled as confidentially as practical.

Enforcement may include:

1. A private correction for a minor or isolated lapse.
2. A formal warning with clear boundaries and consequences.
3. Temporary removal from project interaction for sustained or serious violations.
4. Permanent removal for repeated violations, harassment, threats, or deliberate harm.

Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is itself a violation.
